# dense-mix

"Sixteen Steps" as a busy production: lead, bass, sustained chord pad, 16th-note plucked arpeggio, drums and a dotted-eighth echo (15.4 s). Synthesised by `../_tools/make_audio.py` (fixed random seed for the drums). **MIT**, Lumi Living, no samples.

- `source.wav`: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono.
- `source.mid`: the notes of every part (Lead, Bass, Chords, Arpeggio, Drums on channel 10). The echo is not in the MIDI.

Exercises: ghost notes and harmonics in transcription, chord detection to arpeggios, drum folding, reduction to three voices.
