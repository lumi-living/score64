# ambience-bed

10 s of wind-like noise with slow swells, a low drone (55 and 82.5 Hz) and seven short falling "drips". Synthesised by `../_tools/make_audio.py`, fixed random seed. **MIT**, Lumi Living, no samples.

- `source.wav`: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono. No MIDI: there are no notes to transcribe.

Exercises: the ambience path (gameplay audio made of ambience and foley), and behaviour on material without clear notes.
