# sparse-melody-bass

Lead melody and bass of "Sixteen Steps" (8 bars, A minor, 125 BPM, 15.4 s), synthesised by `../_tools/make_audio.py` from `../_tools/composition.py`. **MIT**, Lumi Living, no samples.

- `source.wav`: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono.
- `source.mid`: the exact notes (format 1, tracks Lead and Bass).

Exercises: transcription of a clean two-part texture, lead selection, octave folding, the tempo grid (the tempo snaps exactly to 6 frames per row). The GoatTracker arrangement is `../gt2-original`.
