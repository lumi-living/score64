# drum-loop

Two bars of kick, snare and closed hat at 125 BPM (3.8 s), synthesised by `../_tools/make_audio.py`: a pitch-swept sine kick, a noise-and-tone snare, a high-passed noise hat, fixed random seed. **MIT**, Lumi Living, no samples.

- `source.wav`: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono.
- `source.mid`: the hits on channel 10 (General MIDI 36, 38, 42).

Exercises: onset detection, beat grid, drum folding into a noise-waveform voice.
